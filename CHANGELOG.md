#### 1.4.1
* With Rockbiter on your weapon, Windfury's DPS and the Total DPS of other imbues no longer count Rockbiter's attack power. The new imbue would replace it.

#### 1.4
* Weapon abilities like Mortal Strike, Sinister Strike, Shred and Aimed Shot now show their lines, worked out from your weapon damage and attack power.
* Heroic Strike, Cleave, Maul and Raptor Strike show "Added DPS": what they add to your swing when used on every one. "Total DPS" of the whole swing is an option in the Lines tab.
* Attack, Auto Shot and wand Shoot show the DPS of your weapons. The details list each hand.
* Windfury, Flametongue, Frostbrand and Rockbiter Weapon show the DPS they add to your main hand.
* Poisons show on their item what they add to each hand. Attack and weapon abilities list the poisons and imbues on your weapons.
* Both can be turned off in the Lines tab.
* "Include crit" counts crits for imbues and poisons too, and leaves out ticks that can't crit, like Lifebloom's or Vampiric Touch's.

#### 1.3.1
* Pet abilities now show their lines on the pet bar and in the pet spellbook.
* Kidney Shot, Slice and Dice and Expose Armor no longer show a lone "Combo points" line.
* Hellfire: the damage you take yourself is no longer counted as the spell's damage.
* Crit: Drain Life, Siphon Life and Shadow Cleave count as spells, not melee. Lightning Shield's charges add up.
* Importing into the current profile replaces it instead of mixing the two.

#### 1.3
* Totems crit again with "Include crit" on. Elemental Fury now counts for Searing and Magma Totem.
* Searing Totem shows DPS and DPM, at one attack every 2.425 sec (from WoWSims). Per hit moved to the details.
* Magma and Healing Stream Totem show DPS and HPS instead of DoT and HoT.
* Amounts like 1815 no longer show as 1815.0.

#### 1.2.1
* "Hold Shift for details" no longer shows on spells with nothing more to show, like Purge or most totems.

#### 1.2
* Totems don't crit anymore with "Include crit" on.
* New Details tab: pick which lines the details show, like the spell school or the crit lines. The details key moved here from the Lines tab.

#### 1.1
* Pressing Shift over an open spellbook tooltip now shows the details right away.
* "Include crit" now counts for pets too: a hunter's pet uses your higher melee or ranged crit, a warlock's pet your spell crit.
